## 📌 Executive Overview

The **Andalusia Healthcare Performance & Financial Intelligence System** is an enterprise-grade hospital analytics suite built in Power BI for **Andalusia Hospitals Group**. Designed for hospital C-suite executives, medical directors, and financial controllers, the dashboard monitors operational throughput, consultation volume (C/V), payer mix, marketing lead conversion, and target revenue realization across specialized medical facilities in **Egypt** and the **Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)**.

Operating across 3 premier hospital business units (**AMH**, **ASH**, and **HJH**) and 10 clinical specialties, this system reconciles clinical activity against baseline models and budgeted targets across **March, April, and May 2025**, tracking **$575.2M in realized revenue** across **174,824 patient encounters**.

## 💡 Key Strategic Insights & Discoveries

1. **The $149.7M Target Gap Bottleneck:**
   - Realized revenue of **$575.2M achieved 79.35%** of the allocated $724.9M target.
   - Crucially, patient encounter volume (**174,824 vs 243,620, 71.76% Ach**) accounted for the vast majority of the shortfall. Consultation value per visit remained robust (**85.76% of target** and **+22.20% growth**), indicating that **patient throughput and clinical scheduling bottlenecks**—not clinical underpricing—drove the variance.

2. **KSA (HJH) Operational Excellence vs Egypt Scaling:**
   - **HJH (Jeddah, KSA)** outperformed all units with **90.92% target achievement** and **+36.86% growth**, leaving a narrow deficit of just -$15.2M.
   - **AMH (Maadi, Egypt)** contributed the highest absolute volume ($235.4M, 78.84% Ach, +30.76% growth).
   - **ASH (Smouha, Egypt)** experienced the steepest operational lag at **72.48% revenue achievement** and only **+8.82% growth**, pointing to local capacity underutilization in Alexandria.

3. **Critical Care & Surgical Super-Performers:**
   - **NICU (Neonatal ICU)** shattered targets, achieving **246.46% of budget** ($11.93M vs $4.84M target, +73.81% growth).
   - **Open Heart Surgery** achieved **181.17% of budget** ($7.49M vs $4.14M target, +27.82% growth).
   - **PICU** achieved **128.29% of budget** ($8.58M).
   - Together, these specialized surgical and intensive care units offset outpatient clinic volume deficits.

4. **April Operational Peak:**
   - **April 2025** recorded an outstanding **98.18% budget achievement** ($242.2M realized vs $246.7M target) and **+69.08% YoY growth**, demonstrating optimal facility occupancy before May seasonal variations.

5. **Heavy Institutional Payer Reliance (85.20% Credit):**
   - With **85.20% of revenue coming through corporate and private health insurers**, cash flow stability heavily depends on medical claim approval velocity, denial management, and insurer reimbursement cycles.

## 📐 Core DAX Measures & Formulas Reference

All business logic is implemented via modular DAX measure groups in the Power BI Semantic Model:

### 1. Financial & Clinical Billing Measures (`Dax Billing`)

```dax
// --- Base Aggregations ---
Revenue Actual = SUM('fact_billing'[Actual Revenue])

Revenue Target = SUM('fact_billing'[Target Revenue])

Revenue Historical = SUM('fact_billing'[Historical Revenue])

Revenue Baseline = SUM('fact_billing'[Baseline Revenue])

Volume Actual = SUM('fact_billing'[Actual Volume])

Volume Target = SUM('fact_billing'[Target Volume])

Volume Historical = SUM('fact_billing'[Historical Volume])

Volume Baseline = SUM('fact_billing'[Baseline Volume])

CPV Actual = SUM('fact_billing'[Actual C/V])

CPV Target = SUM('fact_billing'[Target C/V])

CPV Historical = SUM('fact_billing'[Historical C/V])

CPV Baseline = SUM('fact_billing'[Baseline C/V])

// --- Variance & Achievement Rates ---
Revenue Ach % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Revenue Actual],
    [Revenue Target]
)

Revenue G % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Revenue Actual] - [Revenue Historical],
    [Revenue Historical]
)

Volume Ach % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Volume Actual],
    [Volume Target]
)

Volume G % = 
DIVIDE(
    [Volume Actual] - [Volume Historical],
    [Volume Historical]
)

CPV Ach % = 
DIVIDE(
    [CPV Actual],
    [CPV Target]
)

CPV G % = 
DIVIDE(
    [CPV Actual] - [CPV Historical],
    [CPV Historical]
)
```

### 2. Dynamic Matrix Switcher (`Metrics`)

A disconnected calculated table (`Metrics`) drives dynamic matrix visual pivoting across groups and calculation types:

```dax
Matrix Value = 
VAR _Group = SELECTEDVALUE(Metrics[Group])
VAR _Metric = SELECTEDVALUE(Metrics[Metric])

RETURN
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),

    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "Baseline",   [Revenue Baseline],
    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "Historical", [Revenue Historical],
    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "Actual",     [Revenue Actual],
    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "Target",     [Revenue Target],
    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "Ach %",      [Revenue Ach %],
    _Group = "Revenue" && _Metric = "G %",        [Revenue G %],

    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "Baseline",    [Volume Baseline],
    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "Historical",  [Volume Historical],
    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "Actual",      [Volume Actual],
    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "Target",      [Volume Target],
    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "Ach %",       [Volume Ach %],
    _Group = "Volume" && _Metric = "G %",         [Volume G %],

    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "Baseline",       [CPV Baseline],
    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "Historical",     [CPV Historical],
    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "Actual",         [CPV Actual],
    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "Target",         [CPV Target],
    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "Ach %",          [CPV Ach %],
    _Group = "CPV" && _Metric = "G %",            [CPV G %]
)
```

### 3. Digital Marketing Measures (`Dax Digital Marketing`)

```dax
Ach % Revenue DM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual Revenue]), SUM('fact_marketing'[Target Revenue]))

Ach % Volume DM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual Volume]), SUM('fact_marketing'[Target Volume]))

Ach % RPP DM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual RPP]), SUM('fact_marketing'[Target RPP]))

G % Revenue DM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual Revenue]) - SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline Revenue])), SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline Revenue]))

G % Volume DM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual Volume]) - SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline Volume])), SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline Volume]))

G % RPP DM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_marketing'[Actual RPP]) - SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline RPP])), SUM('fact_marketing'[Baseline RPP]))
```

### 4. Medical CRM Funnel Measures (`Dax Medica CRM`)

```dax
Ach % Revenue MCRM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual Revenue]), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Target Revenue]))

Ach % Volume MCRM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual Volume]), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Target Volume]))

Ach % CPV MCRM = DIVIDE(SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual CPV]), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Target CPV]))

G % Revenue MCRM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual Revenue]) - SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline Revenue])), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline Revenue]))

G % Volume MCRM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual Volume]) - SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline Volume])), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline Volume]))

G % CPV MCRM = DIVIDE((SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Actual CPV]) - SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline CPV])), SUM('fact_medical_crm'[Baseline CPV]))
```

## 🏗️ Data Architecture & Star Schema

The analytics architecture integrates clinical throughput, billing, marketing, and CRM dimensions into a star schema:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────┐
                  │       dim_date       │
                  └──────────┬───────────┘
                             │
       ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
       ▼                     ▼                     ▼
┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐
│    dim_bu    │      │ fact_billing │      │ dim_country  │
└──────┬───────┘      └──────┬───────┘      └──────┬───────┘
       │                     │                     │
       └─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┘
                             │
       ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
       ▼                     ▼                     ▼
┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐
│ dim_medical_ │      │ fact_market- │      │ dim_payment  │
│  department  │      │     ing      │      │              │
└──────────────┘      └──────────────┘      └──────────────┘
```

- **Fact Tables:**
  - `fact_billing` (146 records, 17 fields): Clinical volume, consultation value, revenue actuals, targets, historicals, and baselines across all clinical partitions.
  - `fact_marketing` (9 records, 11 fields): Digital marketing acquisition channel metrics, actual/target revenue, volume, and RPP.
  - `fact_medical_crm` (9 records, 11 fields): CRM lead tracking, appointment throughput, actual/target revenue, volume, and CPV.
- **Dimension Tables:**
  - `dim_bu` (3 records): Hospital business units (`AMH`, `ASH`, `HJH`).
  - `dim_country` (2 records): Country markets (`Egypt`, `KSA`).
  - `dim_medical_department` (10 records): Specialty departments (Cath LAB, ER, Inpatient, Outpatient, ICU, CCU, NICU, PICU, Open Heart, Pharmacy).
  - `dim_payment` (2 records): Settlement types (`Cash`, `Credit`).
  - `dim_date` (3 records): Monthly calendar dates and temporal attributes.
- **Disconnected Matrix Tables:**
  - `Metrics`: Calculated table providing 18 metric/group combinations for dynamic matrix evaluation.
  - `Matrix`: Visual category helper table for tabular matrix layouts.

## ⚙️ ETL & Power Query Data Pipeline

1. **Extraction & Standardization:**
   - Multi-source Excel workbooks (`fact_billing.xlsx`, `dim_*.xlsx`) normalized in Power Query (M).
   - Date keys cast into standard integer surrogate keys (`YYYYMMDD`).
2. **Schema Cleansing & Typing:**
   - Currency fields typed as `Fixed Decimal Number` / `Currency` to prevent floating-point calculation drift.
   - Clinical volume measures typed as `Whole Number` (Integer).
   - Zero-division guardrails applied across all ratio calculations via DAX `DIVIDE()`.
3. **Disconnected Modeling:**
   - Custom DAX table `DATATABLE()` generation for matrix row-swapping without Cartesian product degradation.
